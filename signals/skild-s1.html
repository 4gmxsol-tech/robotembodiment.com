Skild S1 Signal

Skild AI's S1 robot foundation model was highlighted by NVIDIA in September 2026 for single-video task learning.

What changed

NVIDIA reported that Skild AI's S1 can learn previously unseen long-horizon tasks from a single video demonstration without updating model weights or task-specific post-training.

Embodiment relevance

The approach points toward a robot intelligence layer where demonstrations can specify new behavior without a separate training cycle for every task.

Infrastructure

NVIDIA says S1 uses its technologies for synthetic data generation, model training, simulation and real-world deployment.
