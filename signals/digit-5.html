Digit 5 Signal

Agility Robotics unveiled Digit 5 on September 15, 2026 as its next-generation humanoid platform.

What changed

Agility describes Digit 5 as engineered for cooperatively safe work at scale, allowing close-proximity operation with people without the physical safety barriers used by traditional automation.

Deployment context

The company says Digit 5 builds on more than 65,000 hours of operation and commercial deployments of its earlier platform.

Embodiment relevance

Digit is an example of embodied AI being shaped by the requirements of real facilities, where navigation, manipulation, safety and workflow integration meet.
