Trust / Editorial

How we build the reference layer.

Robot Embodiment organizes a fast-moving technical field into source-linked reference material.

Sources

For technical and company claims, we prefer primary research papers, official documentation, official company announcements and other authoritative sources. Secondary sources may provide context when primary material is unavailable.

Scope

Coverage focuses on embodied AI, robot learning, physical intelligence, foundation models, humanoid robotics, manipulation, simulation, data and control.

Corrections

Robotics changes quickly. When a factual error or outdated statement is identified, it should be corrected or clarified rather than silently preserved.

Independence

Directory inclusion is not an endorsement, ranking or investment recommendation. Acquisition information on the site concerns the domain itself and is separate from editorial inclusion.
