Physical platforms.

A framework for cataloguing robots by embodiment, mobility and capability.

Humanoid robots

Human-form platforms designed around environments, tools and workflows built for people.

Mobile & wheeled robots

Robots optimized for navigation, logistics, inspection and other mobile tasks.

Quadruped robots

Legged platforms designed for mobility over varied terrain and access to environments wheels may not handle well.

Manipulation systems

Arms, hands and integrated platforms focused on grasping and physical interaction.
