Robot manipulation.

Manipulation is the problem of making physical contact useful: grasping, moving, placing, opening, assembling and interacting with objects.

The manipulation loop

1. Perceive

Estimate objects, surfaces, pose, contact opportunities and uncertainty.

2. Plan

Select grasps, motions and task sequences while accounting for geometry and constraints.

3. Control

Execute motion and force commands while responding to contact and changes in the environment.

Dexterity

Dexterous manipulation extends this problem to richer contact patterns and more capable hands or end-effectors. It is particularly sensitive to sensing, control bandwidth, data quality and embodiment.

Why it is difficult

The physical world is uncertain. Objects deform, friction varies, perception is incomplete and small errors can cause failure. Successful systems therefore combine modeling, feedback and learning rather than relying on a single technique.
