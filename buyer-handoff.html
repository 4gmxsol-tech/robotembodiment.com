Acquisition / Handoff

Buyer Handoff

The property is structured so a buyer can take over the domain and continue the existing knowledge hub without rebuilding the information architecture from scratch.

Current asset scope

  • RobotEmbodiment.com domain.
  • Static website source and content structure.
  • Research, model, company, robot, signal and directory pages currently published.
  • SEO foundation including canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt and structured data on applicable pages.

Transfer notes

Exact transfer scope is agreed as part of the transaction. Third-party company names, trademarks, logos, external sources and linked materials are not transferred as intellectual property of the seller.

Post-acquisition direction

The buyer can evolve the property into a research publication, robotics intelligence database, company showcase, technical media property, developer resource, or product layer. The current architecture is intentionally static and portable.
