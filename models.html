The robot model layer.

Foundation models and VLA systems are becoming a central layer between multimodal intelligence and robot control.

Gemini Robotics 2

Google DeepMind describes Gemini Robotics 2 as a vision-language-action model that can control different robot embodiments, including full humanoids, and perform tasks involving dexterity and whole-body control.

Source: Google DeepMind — Gemini Robotics 2

Gemini Robotics-ER 2

Google DeepMind describes its embodied-reasoning model as a system for understanding physical spaces and planning multi-step tasks, with motor execution handed to a lower-level VLA model.

Source: Google DeepMind — Gemini Robotics ER 2

NVIDIA Isaac GR00T N1 / N1.5 / N1.6

NVIDIA's GR00T family is an open foundation-model line for generalist humanoid robots. NVIDIA's research pages describe successive improvements in generalization and performance across simulated and real robot settings.

Sources: GR00T N1 research · GR00T N1.6 research

Why this layer matters

A useful way to understand embodied AI is as a stack: perception and reasoning at the top, planning and action generation in the middle, and robot-specific control and hardware underneath. Different research programs place these boundaries differently.
