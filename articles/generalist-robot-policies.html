Generalist Robot Policies Explained

What generalist robot policies are, how they differ from task-specific controllers and why embodiment-aware interfaces matter.

Published September 26, 2026 · Robot Embodiment Editorial

What is a generalist robot policy?

A generalist policy aims to perform multiple tasks or operate across multiple conditions rather than optimizing one fixed behavior.

Why generalization is hard

Tasks vary in objects, goals, environments and physical interactions. A policy must generalize without losing the low-level reliability required by hardware.

Embodiment as an interface

Generalist behavior becomes more reusable when the policy can separate task-level intent from embodiment-specific execution. This connects generalist policies to cross-embodiment learning and robot foundation models.
