Embodied AI Glossary

A practical glossary of terms used across embodied AI, robot learning, VLA models and humanoid robotics.

Embodied AI
AI designed to perceive, reason and act through a physical or simulated body.
VLA
Vision-Language-Action model connecting visual and language inputs to robot actions.
Embodied Reasoning
Reasoning about physical environments, objects, goals and actions.
Robot Foundation Model
A general-purpose model intended to support multiple robot tasks or embodiments.
Sim-to-Real
Transfer of learned behavior from simulation to physical hardware.
Teleoperation
Human control of a robot used for operation, supervision or data collection.
Robot Data
Experience such as demonstrations, trajectories, sensor observations and interaction outcomes.
Manipulation
Physical interaction with objects using robot end effectors.
Dexterous Manipulation
Fine-grained object interaction requiring coordinated hands, fingers or grippers.
Whole-Body Control
Coordinating locomotion, balance and manipulation across the robot body.
Cross-Embodiment
Learning or transferring skills across robots with different bodies and sensors.
Policy
A mapping from observations and state to actions.
Imitation Learning
Learning behavior from demonstrations.
Reinforcement Learning
Learning behavior through interaction and reward signals.
World Model
A learned representation or predictive model of aspects of an environment.
End Effector
The tool or hand at the end of a robot arm.
Degrees of Freedom
Independent motion dimensions available to a robot mechanism.
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
A proposed level of AI with broad, transferable competence across many tasks; definitions and evaluation criteria remain contested.
Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)
A hypothetical AI capability level substantially exceeding human performance across many important cognitive domains.
Embodied AGI
A research direction exploring how general-purpose intelligence could be grounded in perception and action through physical or simulated bodies.
Physical AI
A broad term for AI systems that interact with physical environments through robots or other cyber-physical systems.
Physical Grounding
Connecting abstract symbols, language or predictions to objects, spatial relations, actions and outcomes in an environment.
World Model
A learned representation or predictive model of aspects of an environment.
Predictive State Representation
A state representation defined by predictions about future observations or events.
Affordance Learning
Learning which actions an object or environment makes possible for a particular agent.
Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning
Reinforcement learning with temporally extended skills, options or subpolicies.
Open-World Generalization
Robust performance in environments containing novel objects, tasks or conditions beyond the training distribution.
Continual Learning
Learning new tasks or information over time while retaining previously acquired capabilities.
Cross-Embodiment Transfer
Transferring representations or skills between robots with different morphologies, sensors and action interfaces.
Runtime Assurance
Monitoring and constraining a system during operation to keep behavior within defined safety requirements.
Long-Horizon Planning
Planning sequences of dependent actions over extended time periods while tracking goals and changing state.
Foundation Model
A broadly trained model adapted to downstream tasks.
