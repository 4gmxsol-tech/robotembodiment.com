Digit 5

Next-generation humanoid robot from Agility Robotics for work in warehouses and industrial environments.

Embodiment profile

Agility positions Digit for human-oriented facilities, with applications including material handling and logistics. The company says the platform has accumulated more than 65,000 hours of production experience across deployments.

2026 generation

Agility unveiled Digit 5 on September 15, 2026, describing it as designed for cooperatively safe work at scale. Specifications and features are still subject to change during development.
