Apollo 2

Current humanoid robot platform from Apptronik, developed for real-world work and physical AI data collection.

Embodiment profile

Apptronik describes Apollo as a general-purpose humanoid designed for environments such as warehouses and manufacturing. Apollo 2 is the current platform announced alongside the company's Robot Park data-collection program.

Robot Park

Apptronik says Apollo 2 fleets collect real-world data across Robot Park locations and customer sites to support development of AI models for future humanoid systems.
