NEO

Home humanoid robot platform from 1X, built around real-world learning and compliant physical interaction.

Embodiment profile

1X describes NEO as a home robot using its AI model trained on real-world data. The platform uses tendon-based actuation and a soft-body design intended for interaction around people.

Current availability

1X states that NEO is available for pre-order, with first consumer-home orders shipping in 2026 and broader market expansion planned later.
