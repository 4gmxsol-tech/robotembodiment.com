Walker S2

Third-generation industrial humanoid platform from UBTECH Robotics.

Embodiment profile

Walker S2 is designed for industrial environments and combines perception, navigation, manipulation and task execution within UBTECH's embodied intelligence stack.

Energy autonomy

UBTECH highlights an autonomous hot-swappable battery system. The company states that Walker S2 can perform a battery swap in about three minutes and supports continuous operation through its dual-battery design.
