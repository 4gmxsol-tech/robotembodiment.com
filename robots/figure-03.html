Figure describes F.03 as a home-oriented evolution of its humanoid platform. Its Helix 02 system connects perception, whole-body control and manipulation for long-horizon physical tasks.

Recent deployment

Figure reported that F.03 arrived at BMW Plant Spartanburg in June 2026 for a logistics sequencing workflow, following earlier Figure 02 deployment work.
