Atlas

Electric humanoid robot developed by Boston Dynamics for industrial work and material handling.

Embodiment profile

Atlas is designed for human-scale industrial environments, with autonomous navigation, manipulation, tactile sensing and workflow integrations.

Selected specifications

Boston Dynamics lists 56 degrees of freedom, a 1.9 m height, 90 kg weight, 4-hour battery life and IP67 protection for the current product version.
