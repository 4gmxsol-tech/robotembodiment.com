Walker C1

Full-size service humanoid robot from UBTECH Robotics for multi-scenario environments.

Embodiment profile

UBTECH describes Walker C1 as a service humanoid designed for urban and everyday environments, combining mobility with multimodal intelligence and embodied AI technologies.

System orientation

The platform is presented as a service-oriented counterpart to UBTECH's industrial Walker S family, extending humanoid embodiment toward public and service scenarios.
