Research / Topics

The research frontier.

A structured starting point for understanding the technical problems behind physical intelligence.

Robot Learning

Learning policies and skills from demonstrations, interaction, reinforcement learning and robot datasets.

Perception & State Estimation

Turning cameras, tactile sensors and other observations into representations useful for action.

Planning & Control

Connecting high-level goals to motion, grasping and low-level control under physical constraints.

Simulation & Synthetic Data

Using simulated environments to generate experience, test policies and reduce the cost of physical experimentation.

Robot Data

Collecting and curating physical interaction data remains a central challenge for scalable robot learning.

Generalization

How robots transfer skills across objects, tasks, environments and embodiments is a key research problem.

Editorial note: this page is a topic guide, not a claim that every approach has the same evidence or maturity.
