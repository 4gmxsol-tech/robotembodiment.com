Robot Teleoperation

How human-operated robots generate data, validate tasks and bridge the gap between human skill and autonomous control.

Teleoperation as a data interface

Teleoperation can provide demonstrations in which a human directly produces the behavior a robot should learn. These trajectories can contain observations, actions and task outcomes.

Why it matters

Physical tasks often have complex contact dynamics and long-tail edge cases that are difficult to specify with hand-written programs. Human demonstrations provide a practical route to collecting grounded experience.

Trade-off

Teleoperation can be expensive and may encode operator-specific behavior. Dataset diversity, coverage and quality therefore remain important.
